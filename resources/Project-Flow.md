# CPN-AI — Campus Projects & Proof Network

## Project Overview

**CPN-AI** (Campus Projects & Proof Network) is a comprehensive academic management platform that combines project and research lifecycle management with AI-powered assistance and blockchain-based verification. The platform provides complete workflow management for educational institutions, featuring semester-based course management, RAG-based AI content generation, personalized opportunity recommendations, and immutable verification of academic milestones and achievements.

---

## Problem Statement

Academic institutions face significant challenges in managing educational workflows and student opportunities:

- **Fragmented Academic Management**: Disconnected tools for course management, project tracking, and resource organization
- **Limited AI Integration**: Lack of intelligent assistance for research, content generation, and personalized student guidance
- **Poor Opportunity Discovery**: Students miss relevant jobs, competitions, and higher study opportunities due to lack of personalized matching
- **Manual Course Resource Management**: Time-intensive manual organization of course materials without intelligent content generation
- **Lack of Verification**: No immutable proof of academic achievements, submissions, and milestone completions
- **Limited Collaboration**: Poor support for semester-based project collaboration between teachers and students
- **Scattered Announcements**: Fragmented systems for sharing achievements, opportunities, and institutional communications

---

## Solution Description

CPN-AI addresses these challenges by providing an **AI-enhanced academic management platform** that combines comprehensive course and project management with intelligent assistance systems and blockchain verification. The platform enables institutions to manage semester-based courses, projects, and research with complete transparency, AI-powered content generation, and personalized student guidance.

### Key Innovation

- **RAG-Based AI Integration**: Advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation for research assistance, content creation, and intelligent course material analysis
- **Personalized Recommendation Engine**: AI-powered matching system that suggests relevant opportunities based on individual student profiles and backgrounds
- **Blockchain Verification**: Immutable proof of submissions, milestones, and achievements using cryptographic hashing
- **Unified Academic Ecosystem**: Integrated platform combining course management, project collaboration, meeting coordination, and opportunity discovery
- **Department-Scoped Management**: Role-based access control ensuring proper organizational structure and academic workflow management

## Core Features

### 🔐 **Role-Based Authentication & User Management**

- **Department Admin User Creation**: Department administrators have exclusive authority to add teachers and students to their specific department
- **Automated Email Credential Delivery**: Login credentials are automatically generated and sent to new users' email addresses upon account creation
- **Role-Based Access Control**: Three distinct user roles (Department Admin, Faculty/Teacher, Student) with department-scoped permissions and access levels
- **Department-Scoped User Management**: All user accounts are tied to specific departments, ensuring proper organizational structure and access control

### 🏛️ **Comprehensive Institution Management**

- **Semester Course Creation**: Department admins create all semester courses with complete academic calendar management and course specifications
- **Teacher & Student Enrollment**: Department admins enroll teachers and students into specific courses based on academic requirements and capacity
- **Automated Email Notifications**: Upon enrollment, both teachers and students automatically receive email notifications confirming their enrollment in courses
- **Course Assignment Management**: Complete control over who teaches and who studies in each course within the department
- **Academic Calendar Integration**: Semester-based course scheduling with start/end date management and enrollment periods
- **Enrollment Tracking**: Real-time monitoring of course enrollment status and capacity management

### 📚 **Unified Course & Resource Management Platform**

- **Centralized Course Dashboard**: Students and teachers access all course-related content, activities, and resources through a unified platform interface
- **Teacher Resource Management**: Teachers can upload, organize, and manage comprehensive course materials including documents, videos, external links, and multimedia content
- **Student Resource Access**: Students can view, download, and interact with all course resources with intelligent categorization and search capabilities
- **RAG-Based AI Content Generation**: Advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation system that analyzes course resources to automatically create relevant quizzes and course content
- **Intelligent Quiz Creation**: AI system generates contextual quizzes based on uploaded course materials and learning objectives
- **Dynamic Content Adaptation**: Course content automatically adapts and expands based on existing resources and student learning patterns
- **Content Organization**: Structured resource management by course topics, modules, and learning objectives with version control
- **Interactive Learning Experience**: Students can ask questions about course materials and receive AI-generated responses based on course resources

### 📋 **Semester-Based Project & Research Management**

- **Teacher-Led Project Creation**: Teachers create course projects within specific semesters with detailed project specifications and learning objectives
- **Teacher-Led Research Creation**: Teachers initiate research activities and define research scope, methodology, and expected outcomes
- **Course-Specific Student Invitations**: Teachers invite students enrolled in their specific courses to collaborate on projects and research activities
- **Dual-Track Academic Workflows**: Separate management systems for course projects and independent research with dedicated interfaces
- **Collaborative Workspace Integration**: Seamless collaboration environment where teachers and students work together on academic activities
- **Project & Research Lifecycle Management**: Complete tracking from initial creation through milestone completion to final assessment and evaluation
- **Student Team Formation**: Teachers assign individual students or form collaborative teams for complex projects and research work
- **Cross-Course Integration**: Ability to create interdisciplinary projects involving students from multiple courses within the semester

### 🎯 **Milestone-Driven Task & Meeting Management**

- **Hierarchical Academic Structure**: Semester → Course → Project/Research → Milestones → Tasks with clear academic progression
- **Milestone Creation & Management**: Each project and research activity divided into specific milestones with defined deliverables and assessment criteria
- **Task Assignment Under Milestones**: Granular task creation, assignment, and tracking within each milestone for detailed progress monitoring
- **Integrated Meeting Scheduling**: Built-in meeting management system for milestone review meetings, project discussions, and research consultations
- **AI-Powered Meeting Summarization**: Automatic generation of meeting summaries, action items, and key decisions using advanced AI technology
- **Task Submission & Review System**: Streamlined submission process with file uploads, external links, and comprehensive review workflows
- **Milestone Progress Tracking**: Real-time monitoring of milestone completion with visual progress indicators and completion percentages
- **Meeting-Task Integration**: Automatic creation of follow-up tasks and action items based on meeting discussions and decisions

### 🔗 **Blockchain-Verified Academic Assessment System**

- **Comprehensive Activity Verification**: Every academic activity including task submissions, milestone completions, and meeting outcomes cryptographically verified
- **Student Progress Blockchain Layer**: All student activities, submissions, and achievements are hashed (SHA-256) and recorded for immutable tracking
- **Milestone Achievement Verification**: Complete milestone verification with aggregated proof of all contained tasks and activities
- **Student Assessment Integration**: Blockchain verification supports comprehensive student assessment by providing tamper-proof evidence of academic engagement
- **Progress Tracking Transparency**: Verifiable timestamps and activity authenticity enabling accurate assessment of student participation and performance
- **Academic Integrity Assurance**: Immutable records prevent academic misconduct and provide reliable evidence for grading and evaluation
- **Downloadable Achievement Certificates**: Students receive blockchain-verified certificates for milestone completions, project achievements, and research contributions

- **Popular Platform Integration**: Seamless integration with popular meeting platforms including Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and other video conferencing tools

- **Cross-Platform Meeting Management**: Unified interface to schedule, manage, and track meetings regardless of the platform used (Zoom, Google Meet, etc.)

## Technical Architecture

### **Frontend Stack**

- **Framework**: Next.js 15 with React 19
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with shadcn/ui components
- **Language**: TypeScript for type safety
- **State Management**: Zustand for global state
- **Authentication**: JWT-based authentication with middleware
- **Data Visualization**: Recharts for analytics and progress tracking
- **Animations**: Framer Motion for smooth user interactions

### **Backend Stack**

- **Runtime**: Node.js with Express.js framework
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM for type-safe database operations
- **Authentication**: JWT-based authentication with role-based access control
- **File Storage**: S3-compatible storage system for document and resource uploads
- **API Design**: RESTful API architecture with comprehensive endpoint documentation
- **Blockchain Integration**: Cryptographic hashing and optional blockchain transaction recording

### **Core Platform Features**

- **Project Management**: Complete project lifecycle with milestone and task tracking
- **Verification System**: SHA-256 hashing and blockchain proof generation
- **Multi-User Collaboration**: Support for team projects and supervisor oversight
- **Resource Management**: Comprehensive file upload and resource organization
- **Meeting Coordination**: Integrated scheduling and meeting management for projects

## Workflow Examples

### 📋 **Course & Project Management Workflow**

1. **Department Setup**: Department admin creates semester courses and enrolls teachers and students
2. **Resource Upload**: Teachers upload course materials for RAG-based AI content generation
3. **Project Creation**: Teachers create course projects and research activities within semesters
4. **Student Invitation**: Teachers invite enrolled students to collaborate on specific projects
5. **Milestone Planning**: Teachers define project milestones with specific deliverables
6. **Task Assignment**: Projects broken down into tasks assigned to individual students or teams
7. **Work Execution**: Students work on tasks with progress tracking and submission capabilities
8. **AI Assistance**: Students use RAG-based research assistant for project guidance
9. **Submission & Review**: Students submit work with blockchain verification; teachers review and provide feedback

### 🤖 **RAG-Based Research Assistant Workflow**

1. **Document Upload**: Students upload research papers to the RAG-based system
2. **Content Analysis**: AI system processes and indexes the uploaded research documents
3. **Query Interaction**: Students ask specific questions about the uploaded papers
4. **Contextual Responses**: AI provides insights and analysis based on document content
5. **Research Guidance**: System offers methodology support and academic guidance
6. **Continuous Learning**: Students build knowledge through iterative questioning and AI responses

### 📢 **Announcement Management Workflow**

1. **Content Creation**: Department admins create announcements for achievements, competitions, jobs, higher studies, and research opportunities
2. **Publication**: Announcements published to appropriate sections within the platform
3. **Student Access**: Students browse and access relevant opportunities through unified interface
4. **Application/Participation**: Students engage with external links for applications or participation

### 🎯 **AI-Powered Personalized Recommendation Workflow**

1. **Profile Building**: System analyzes student's academic background, project history, skills, and course performance
2. **Interest Assessment**: AI evaluates student preferences based on project choices, research areas, and engagement patterns
3. **Opportunity Analysis**: System continuously scans available jobs, competitions, higher studies, and research opportunities
4. **Smart Matching**: AI matching algorithm compares student profiles with opportunity requirements and preferences
5. **Personalized Suggestions**: Students receive tailored recommendations ranked by relevance and fit
6. **Feedback Integration**: Student interactions and selections improve future recommendation accuracy
7. **Career Path Guidance**: AI provides strategic recommendations to help students achieve their professional goals
